## Full description (4000 char max)

InboxIQ is your SMS app, rebuilt to sort itself.

Every text you get — from banks, delivery services, two-factor codes, spam,
scam attempts, and actual people you know — lands in the same inbox looking
identical. InboxIQ automatically sorts them into Personal, Work, Promo,
Verification Codes, Spam, and Suspected Scam, so you can see what actually
needs your attention at a glance.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

• 100% on-device classification. Your messages are never sent to a server —
ours or anyone else's — for sorting. The machine learning model that
categorizes your texts runs entirely on your phone.

• Full SMS and MMS support. InboxIQ is a complete replacement for your
default messaging app, not an add-on — send, receive, and view photo
messages right in the app.

• Know what needs a reply. InboxIQ flags conversations that are waiting on
you, and offers quick-reply suggestions so you can respond in one tap.

• See through spam and scams. Verification codes, promotional texts, and
suspected scam messages are labeled clearly so you're not left guessing
whether that "your account is locked" text is real.

• Dark mode, contact names, and a clean, modern inbox — the basics done
right.

YOUR PRIVACY

InboxIQ was built around a simple rule: your text messages are yours. There
is no cloud AI service involved in reading or categorizing your messages.
Your message content, contacts, and phone numbers stay on your device.
Full privacy policy: https://kreativekoala.llc/privacy

InboxIQ requests SMS, MMS, and Contacts permissions because it is a full
default SMS app — these permissions are what let it send and receive your
texts and show contact names instead of raw phone numbers, nothing more.
