InboxIQ is your SMS app, rebuilt to sort itself.

Every text you get — from banks, delivery services, two-factor codes, spam, scam attempts, and actual people you know — lands in the same inbox looking identical. InboxIQ automatically sorts them into Personal, Work, Promo, Verification Codes, Spam, and Suspected Scam, so you can see what actually needs your attention at a glance.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

• 100% on-device classification. Your messages are never sent to a server — ours or anyone else's — for sorting. The machine learning model that categorizes your texts runs entirely on your phone.

• SMS and MMS in one app. InboxIQ works as your default messaging app for texts and picture messages: send, receive, and view photos right in the app. It does not support RCS chat features (typing indicators, read receipts, Wi-Fi chats) or group picture-message conversations.

• Know what needs a reply. InboxIQ flags conversations that are waiting on you, and offers quick-reply suggestions so you can respond in one tap.

• See through spam and scams. Verification codes, promotional texts, and suspected scam messages are labeled clearly so you're not left guessing whether that "your account is locked" text is real.

• Dark mode, contact names, and a clean, modern inbox — the basics done right.

YOUR PRIVACY

InboxIQ was built around a simple rule: your text messages are yours. There is no cloud AI service involved in reading or categorizing your messages. Your message content, contacts, and phone numbers stay on your device. Full privacy policy: https://kreativekoala.llc/privacy

Permissions, and why: SMS and MMS let InboxIQ receive, send and read your texts and picture messages, which any default SMS app must do. Contacts shows a saved contact's name instead of a raw phone number. Notifications alert you to new messages. The microphone is used only while you hold the voice-memo button; the audio is turned into text on your phone and is not saved or sent. Nothing more.
