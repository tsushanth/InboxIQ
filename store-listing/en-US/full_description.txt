InboxIQ is a text-messaging (SMS and MMS) app that sorts your inbox for you, on your phone.

Every text you get, from banks, delivery services, verification codes, spam, scam attempts and people you know, lands in one inbox looking the same. InboxIQ files each conversation as Personal, Work, Transaction, Promo, Code, Spam or Scam, so you can see what needs your attention at a glance.

WHAT IT DOES

• Replaces your default messaging app: send, receive and view text and picture messages. It does not support RCS chat features (typing indicators, read receipts, Wi-Fi chats) or group picture-message conversations.
• Sorts messages on your phone with a small model that runs entirely on the device. Message text is never sent anywhere to be sorted.
• Shows what needs a reply: it flags conversations that are waiting on you and offers quick-reply suggestions.
• Makes spam and scams easy to spot: spam and suspected scams go to Junk, and risky-looking links get a warning before you open them.
• Verification codes get a Copy code button.
• Block numbers, search messages, dark mode, and contact names instead of raw numbers.
• Voice memos: hold the button to dictate. The audio is turned into text on your phone (English only) and is not saved or sent.

YOUR PRIVACY

InboxIQ was built around a simple rule: your text messages are yours. There is no cloud AI service involved in reading or categorizing your messages. Your message content, contacts, and phone numbers stay on your device. Full privacy policy: https://kreativekoala.llc/privacy

Permissions, and why: SMS and MMS let InboxIQ receive, send and read your texts and picture messages, which any default SMS app must do. Contacts shows a saved contact's name instead of a raw phone number. Notifications alert you to new messages. The microphone is used only while you hold the voice-memo button. Nothing more.
