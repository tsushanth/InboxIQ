InboxIQ is a full text-messaging (SMS and MMS) app that sorts your inbox for you, on your phone.

Every text you get - from banks, delivery services, verification codes, spam, scam attempts, and people you know - lands in one inbox looking the same. InboxIQ sorts them into Personal, Work, Promo, Verification Codes, Spam, and Suspected Scam, so you can see what needs your attention.

WHAT IT DOES

- Replaces your default messaging app: send, receive, and view text and photo messages.
- Sorts messages on your phone. A small classifier runs entirely on-device, so message text is never sent anywhere to be sorted. You can optionally download a larger on-device model through Google Play for more nuanced sorting; it works the same way, locally.
- Flags conversations waiting on a reply and offers quick-reply suggestions.
- Labels verification codes, promotions, and suspected scams, and warns you about risky-looking links.
- Block numbers, search messages, dark mode, and contact names instead of raw numbers.
- Voice memos: hold a button to dictate, transcribed on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

InboxIQ does not have internet access. It does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your messages, contacts, or phone numbers off your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your messages, numbers, and contact names are stored in a database on your phone; uninstalling deletes them.

The optional larger model is delivered by Google Play itself, not by the app, and contains nothing from your phone.

PERMISSIONS

SMS and MMS: required because InboxIQ is your default SMS app (send, receive, and store your texts). Contacts: shows names instead of numbers. Notifications: alerts for new messages. Microphone: voice memos, transcribed on-device.
